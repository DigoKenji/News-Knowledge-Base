Which Claude model should your team use, and when?

Opus 5.5 covers everyday coding and knowledge work, like debugging a release or redlining a contract. Fable 5.2 is for your hardest, most open-ended projects, like large migrations or architecture planning. Sonnet 5.5 fits well-scoped tasks where speed and usage matter. Haiku 5.5 handles high-volume work and subagents through the API. Mythos 5.2 is for cybersecurity and advanced biology.
