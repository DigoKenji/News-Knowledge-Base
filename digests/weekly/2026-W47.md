**1. [$0.0001 Decision Model That Will Replace Millions of LLM Calls](/notes/ai-ml/2026-11-17-0-0001-decision-model-that-will-replace-millions-of-llm-call.md)** — Medium · ai-ml · your saved list
Small typed decision models with calibrated confidence should front agent workflows, escalating only uncertain cases to frontier LLMs.

- Validate vendor latency and cost claims on your own workload before relying on them.

**2. [The Complete Guide to Agent Harnesses (With Code)](/notes/ai-ml/2026-11-20-the-complete-guide-to-agent-harnesses-with-code.md)** — Medium · ai-ml · your saved list
Harness code, not the model, governs agent cost and rule enforcement, so enforce rules in code.
- A rule in a hook stopped the unapproved edit that the same rule in a prompt let through eleven times.
- The same agent and model cost up to 40x more tokens on one harness than another; a model upgrade moved it 1.0 to 1.3x.
- Harness gains often vanish on new tasks: GEPA fell from 70.8% on its tuning set to 54.5% on the wider set.
[Deep dive](https://app.notion.com/p/41ebaf74172d81b1866ecfc255dd007c)

**3. [Self-Improvement Memory Enables Continual Recursive Self-Improvement](/notes/ai-ml/2026-11-17-self-improvement-memory-enables-continual-recursive-self-imp.md)** — arXiv · ai-ml · forwarded by you
Recording why each harness edit was made, and what it predicted and achieved, lets continued optimization beat harness-only transfer.
- A harness evolved for one model or benchmark can start below the seed on another.
- In the paired coding study, SIM won 9 of 10 pairs (mean +2.36 points, p = 0.003).
- Evidence is strongest for coding; other domains rest on single runs.
[Deep dive](https://app.notion.com/p/41cbaf74172d816e920dc492fef2d65e)

**4. [OpenAI Just Open-Sourced Symphony. Every AI Startup Should Pay Attention](/notes/ai-ml/2026-11-20-openai-just-open-sourced-symphony-every-ai-startup-should-pa.md)** — Medium · ai-ml · your saved list
Agent-assisted engineering is shifting from supervising sessions to managing work, with ticket and repo quality deciding the outcome.

- Ticket statuses act as a state machine that drives the orchestrator.
- Repo readiness (structure, tests, guardrails) matters more than the model choice, according to the author.

**5. [Spec-Driven Development Frameworks a Year Later](/notes/software-engineering/2026-11-20-spec-driven-development-frameworks-a-year-later-pattern-fami.md)** — Medium · software-engineering · your saved list
No spec framework has won; the right pattern depends on the change type, and spec maintenance after a second change is unmeasured.
- For a bounded bug fix with a failing test, no spec merged 9 of 10 tickets, as good as the best frameworks.
- For a feature in an existing codebase, spec arms merged 2 to 3 more tickets out of 10.
- 81.5% of spec files in public repos have a single commit, so specs go stale after the first change.
