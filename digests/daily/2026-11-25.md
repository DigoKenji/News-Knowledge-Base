**1. Como eficiência e a disrupção podem acabar com a concentração de mercados que pareciam imutáveis** — Blog · architecture
Shows how opening a closed market moves competition, and why leaders should act before regulation arrives.
- Market concentration is not automatically inefficiency; it can reflect a protected structural barrier rather than superior efficiency.

**2. DoorDash Dispatch: Building a Lightning-Fast Delivery System** — Blog · backend-systems
Shows how to scale a latency-critical optimization system by separating coordination, decomposing work, and migrating safely.
- Dispatch must weigh space and time together, and decisions must be both good and fast enough to be useful.
- Separating a coordinator tier from a compute tier, with localized payload sharding, lets computation scale without moving the state.
- Shadow testing validates live behavior, and switchback experiments measure real impact before a full rollout.

**3. How OpenAI’s Performance Factory keeps ChatGPT fast** — Blog · backend-systems
Shows how agentic workflows can automate production performance work, with the review and evaluation loops needed to trust them.
- Task-based workflows where agents pick up ready work, store state outside their context, and sleep until events arrive.
- Adversarial review by a separate agent reopens weak diagnoses; one caught an unexplained continuing failure.
- Evaluation uses backtests on past incidents, scoring timing, size, affected requests and cause separately.
[Deep dive](https://app.notion.com/p/423baf74172d811d82b4d44fdb14e40f) · [Original](https://openai.com/index/how-openai-performance-factory-keeps-chatgpt-fast)

**4. After waterfall, agile, and DevOps — Anthropic’s AI-Native SDLC playbook** — Medium · software-engineering
A concrete blueprint for redesigning SDLC handoffs and governance around AI agents, from an Anthropic-authored playbook.
- Each SDLC phase commits a git file (intent, spec, plan, code, incident record) that humans and agents both read.
- Skills make compliance probable and hooks make it certain, so policy binds when work is written, not reviewed.
- Production sign-off stays human, autonomy is tiered by environment, and monitoring escalates authority by deviation size.
