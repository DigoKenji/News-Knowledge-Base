- [ ] **E02 · [Architecture as options](/concepts/architecture/architecture-options.md)** — Seeing architecture as buying options - the right, but not the obligation, to make a change later at a known cost - so that teams can defer irreversible decisions until they know more; options are worth most when uncertainty is high.
  - Sources: 3 (the book + 2 independent)

- [ ] **E03 · [Architecture diagrams and diagram-driven design](/concepts/architecture/architecture-diagrams.md)** — Using diagrams not only to communicate an architecture but to test it - a good diagram shows components, the relationships (lines) between them and the decisions behind them, at a level of detail chosen for its audience.
  - Sources: 3 (the book + 2 independent)
