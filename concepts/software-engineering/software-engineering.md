# Software engineering

**Definition.** ISO/IEC/IEEE 24765 and the IEEE SWEBOK define software engineering as the application of a systematic, disciplined, quantifiable approach to the development, operation and maintenance of software. The term was popularised at the 1968 NATO conference, called to address the "software crisis" of late, buggy, over-budget projects.

Domain: [software-engineering](/domains/software-engineering.md) · subdomain `core`. Core vocabulary entry (approved seed list, 2026-11-24); draft waiting for Kenji to certify.

## How it fits

It is the umbrella for everything in this theme: life cycles, requirements, design, construction, testing, maintenance, configuration management and engineering management (the SWEBOK knowledge areas). It differs from programming by its focus on teams, time and change: most software cost is in maintaining and evolving code, not writing it the first time.

## Example

A student writes a script that works once; a software engineering team builds a payment system with requirements, design reviews, tests, CI/CD, monitoring and a plan for the next five years of changes.
