# Architecture

Architecture as Kenji practises it and studies it in his MBA. Subdomains follow TOGAF: four **domains** (business,
data, application, technology) and two **scopes** (enterprise, solution), plus three practices. TOGAF treats enterprise
and solution as scopes, not domains; see [Architecture domain](/foundations/architecture-domain.md).

## Subdomains

The controlled list for notes and concepts in this domain (taxonomy, 2026-11-26), based on SWEBOK v4 (Software Architecture knowledge area) and TOGAF 9.2 (enterprise architecture domains and scopes). A concept has exactly one home subdomain and may be cross-listed elsewhere with `also_in`.

- `enterprise-architecture` — **Enterprise architecture**: Scope: the whole enterprise (strategy, portfolio, governance, ADM)
- `business-architecture` — **Business architecture**: Domain: capabilities, value streams, organization, information, processes (BIZBOK, BPMN)
- `data-architecture` — **Data architecture**: Domain: data assets and data management resources
- `application-architecture` — **Application architecture**: Domain: applications, their interactions and the capabilities they support
- `technology-architecture` — **Technology architecture**: Domain: technology services and components (infrastructure, platforms)
- `solution-architecture` — **Solution architecture**: Scope: one business operation, project or release and how IT supports it
- `software-architecture` — **Software architecture**: Practice: structure of one system - styles, patterns, integration, quality attributes, modular boundaries
- `architecture-practice` — **Architecture practice**: Practice: how architects work - decisions and their records, evaluation, diagrams, the role
- `architecture-governance` — **Architecture governance**: Practice: principles, compliance, architecture boards, EA capability
