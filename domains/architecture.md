# Architecture

Architecture as Kenji practises it and studies it in his MBA. Subdomains follow TOGAF: four **domains** (business,
data, application, technology) and two **scopes** (enterprise, solution), plus two practices. TOGAF treats enterprise
and solution as scopes, not domains; see [Architecture domain](/foundations/architecture-domain.md).

## Subdomains
- `enterprise-architecture` — Scope: the whole enterprise (strategy, portfolio, governance, ADM)
- `business-architecture` — Domain: capabilities, value streams, organization, information, processes (BIZBOK, BPMN)
- `data-architecture` — Domain: data assets and data management resources
- `application-architecture` — Domain: applications, their interactions and the capabilities they support
- `technology-architecture` — Domain: technology services and components (infrastructure, platforms)
- `solution-architecture` — Scope: one business operation, project or release and how IT supports it
- `integration-and-software-architecture` — Practice: integration patterns, application and software structure
- `architecture-governance` — Practice: principles, compliance, architecture boards, EA capability
